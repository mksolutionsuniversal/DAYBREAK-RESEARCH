# DAYBREAK RESEARCH evidence standard

| Status | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| CONFIRMED | Finding documented within a defined scope by an identified source. |
| PROBABLE | Available evidence supports the inference, but direct conclusive evidence is absent. |
| UNKNOWN | Available data or methods do not resolve the question. |

The status applies to a specific claim, not an entire incident. We distinguish static analysis (code properties), dynamic analysis (recorded behaviour), examination of preserved artefacts and operator accounts. Temporal correlation does not establish causation.

Launching a network process, attempting a connection and receiving data represent distinct levels of evidence. Copying a database does not establish access to its records; matching hashes do not establish monitoring completeness. Failure to find a synthetic marker does not establish that the application was not accessed.

Times are reported in UTC, source identifiers are retained within each publication and network indicators are defanged. Public materials do not replace the complete private corpus. Corrections should preserve version history and identify their basis.
